Trial Guardian privacy policy

Last updated: TODO-release-date

Trial Guardian helps you cancel free trials before you are charged. It was built so that it does not need your personal data, and it does not collect any.

What stays on your iPhone

The trials and subscriptions you save (service name, price, dates, any cancel link, notes and proof photos you attach) and your reminder settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you turn on iCloud sync, a copy of your trials (not your proof photos) is kept in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your other devices can show them. We cannot see it. Backups and CSV exports are files you create and share yourself.

Add to Calendar opens Apple's own event sheet; Trial Guardian does not read your calendar. The Face ID lock uses Apple's Face ID, and Trial Guardian never receives your face data. Siri and Shortcuts read a small summary of your upcoming trials stored on your device.

Text and screenshots you share

When you share an email, text or screenshot to Trial Guardian, it is read on your iPhone to find the trial details. Screenshots are read with Apple's on-device text recognition, and text may be read with Apple Intelligence's on-device model where your iPhone supports it. Nothing you share is uploaded, and the original text or image is not kept after you save the trial.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The Lock Screen countdown and widgets are drawn on your device from the trials you saved. No push notification service is used.

What Trial Guardian does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No access to your email inbox, bank or cards.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No servers of our own: the app works fully offline.

Links you open

If you tap a cancel link, it opens the service's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

Trial Guardian is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
