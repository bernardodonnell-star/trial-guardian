How do I add a trial?

In Mail, Photos or Safari tap Share, then Trial Guardian. If you don't see it, scroll the app row to the end, tap More and turn Trial Guardian on. You can also open the app and tap Add a trial to paste text, choose a screenshot or type the details.

Why didn't it find the date or price?

Some emails describe the trial in an image or leave details out. Anything not found is marked so you can fill it in before saving.

I'm not getting reminders

Check Settings, Notifications, Trial Guardian and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

When does the Lock Screen countdown appear?

iOS lets a countdown run for up to eight hours, so it starts during the final hours before the charge day, when you open the app or tap the last-evening reminder. The widget shows the next trial at any time.

Does Trial Guardian cancel trials for me?

No. It reminds you and opens the cancel page or your App Store subscriptions. You stay in control.
